Research Areas

Advancing energy storage through innovative materials and advanced characterization

Our Research Philosophy

Our research integrates materials synthesis, electrochemical engineering, and advanced characterization to address fundamental challenges in energy storage. We focus on three interconnected areas that together advance the development of next-generation batteries.

🔋

All-Solid-State Batteries (ASSBs)

Overview

All-solid-state batteries represent the next frontier in energy storage technology, offering enhanced safety and potentially higher energy density compared to conventional liquid electrolyte systems. Our research focuses on addressing the critical challenges at solid-solid interfaces that currently limit ASSB performance.

Key Research Topics

  • Solid Electrolyte Interface Engineering: Developing strategies to improve interfacial contact and reduce resistance between solid electrolytes and electrode materials
  • Sulfide-Based Solid Electrolytes: Investigating high ionic conductivity sulfide electrolytes and their stability with lithium metal anodes
  • Interface Characterization: Employing operando techniques to understand interface evolution during battery cycling
  • Composite Cathode Design: Optimizing three-phase (active material, electrolyte, conductive additive) composite cathodes for enhanced performance

Technical Approach

We combine materials synthesis with advanced electrochemical testing and multi-scale characterization. Our operando X-ray techniques allow us to observe interface evolution in real-time, providing insights that guide materials design and processing optimization.

⚛️

Lithium-Sulfur (Li-S) Battery Systems

Overview

Lithium-sulfur batteries offer theoretical energy densities far exceeding current lithium-ion technology. However, challenges including polysulfide shuttle effect and poor sulfur utilization have hindered commercialization. Our research addresses these challenges through novel cathode architectures and electrolyte design.

Key Research Topics

  • Polysulfide Management: Developing host materials and electrolyte additives to suppress polysulfide dissolution and shuttle
  • Cathode Architecture: Designing porous carbon structures and functional coatings to improve sulfur loading and utilization
  • Operando Spectroscopy: Using synchrotron X-ray absorption spectroscopy to track sulfur speciation during cycling
  • Solid-State Li-S Systems: Exploring solid electrolytes as a pathway to eliminate polysulfide shuttle in Li-S batteries

Technical Approach

Our operando characterization capabilities enable direct observation of sulfur chemistry during battery operation. This real-time insight guides the development of materials and cell designs that address fundamental Li-S battery challenges.

⚗️

Anode-Free Batteries & Multi-Scale Characterization

Overview

Anode-free battery architectures eliminate the anode active material, relying on in-situ lithium plating during charging. This approach maximizes energy density but requires precise control of lithium deposition morphology. Our research combines novel cell designs with advanced 3D imaging to understand and control lithium metal electrodeposition.

Key Research Topics

  • Anode-Free Cell Design: Developing current collector surfaces and electrolyte formulations that promote uniform lithium deposition
  • 3D X-ray Tomography: Non-destructive 3D visualization of lithium metal morphology and dendrite formation
  • Operando XRD and XAS: Real-time tracking of structural and electronic changes in battery materials during operation
  • Multi-Scale Integration: Combining microscopy, spectroscopy, and diffraction to build comprehensive understanding from atomic to device scales

Technical Approach

We have developed specialized operando cells compatible with synchrotron X-ray techniques, enabling us to characterize batteries under realistic operating conditions. Our 3D tomography capabilities reveal lithium morphology evolution, while spectroscopy provides chemical and electronic state information.

Advanced Characterization Techniques

Operando X-ray Absorption Spectroscopy (XAS)

Real-time probing of oxidation states and local atomic structure during battery cycling. Provides element-specific information on redox processes and structural evolution.

Operando X-ray Diffraction (XRD)

In-situ monitoring of crystallographic phase changes and lattice parameter evolution. Reveals structural transformations and strain development in electrode materials.

3D X-ray Computed Tomography

Non-destructive 3D imaging of battery microstructure. Visualizes lithium metal morphology, crack formation, and particle fracture at micron resolution.

Electrochemical Impedance Spectroscopy (EIS)

Frequency-domain analysis of battery processes. Separates contributions from different interfaces and transport phenomena to identify performance-limiting factors.

Collaborations & Facilities

Our research leverages world-class facilities at the National Synchrotron Radiation Research Center (NSRRC) in Taiwan. We maintain active collaborations with leading research groups in battery science, materials characterization, and computational modeling both domestically and internationally.
