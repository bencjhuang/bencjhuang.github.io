Biography

Professor Huang joined the Department of Chemical Engineering at Taiwan Tech in January 2026. His research focuses on advanced battery materials and multi-scale characterization techniques, with particular emphasis on all-solid-state batteries, lithium-sulfur systems, and operando synchrotron X-ray methods.

Prior to joining Taiwan Tech, Professor Huang conducted research on next-generation battery technologies and advanced characterization methods. His work bridges fundamental materials science with practical energy storage engineering, combining materials synthesis, electrochemical testing, and state-of-the-art characterization.

Research Interests

  • All-Solid-State Battery Materials and Interfaces
  • Lithium-Sulfur Battery Systems
  • Anode-Free Lithium Metal Batteries
  • Operando Synchrotron X-ray Spectroscopy and Imaging
  • 3D X-ray Computed Tomography of Battery Materials
  • Electrochemical Interface Engineering
