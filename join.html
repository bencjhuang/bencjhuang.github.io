Opportunities for talented researchers to advance battery science

We are looking for motivated and talented researchers to join our team. The CJHuang Group offers a collaborative and intellectually stimulating environment where you can develop your research skills while working on cutting-edge battery technologies and advanced characterization methods.

Open Positions

Updated: February 2026

Postdoctoral Researcher

Position Available
Number of Positions: 1
Start Date: Flexible (2026)
Duration: 2-3 years (renewable)

Position Description

We are seeking a postdoctoral researcher with expertise in battery materials characterization and/or synchrotron X-ray techniques. The successful candidate will lead research projects on all-solid-state batteries, lithium-sulfur systems, or anode-free architectures, while mentoring graduate students and developing independent research directions.

Required Qualifications

  • PhD in Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemistry, Physics, or related field
  • Strong background in battery materials and electrochemistry
  • Experience with materials characterization techniques
  • Track record of publications in peer-reviewed journals
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills in English

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with synchrotron X-ray techniques (XAS, XRD, tomography)
  • Knowledge of solid-state battery systems or lithium metal batteries
  • Familiarity with operando characterization methods
  • Experience mentoring graduate or undergraduate students

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary according to Taiwan Tech standards
  • Access to state-of-the-art facilities and equipment
  • Collaborative environment with domestic and international partners
  • Opportunities for synchrotron beamtime at NSRRC
  • Support for conference travel and professional development

PhD Student Positions

Positions Available
Number of Positions: 1-2
Start Date: September 2026
Funding: Full funding including tuition and stipend

Position Description

We are recruiting motivated PhD students to conduct research on advanced battery materials and characterization. Students will have opportunities to work with synchrotron facilities, publish in high-impact journals, and present at international conferences. All positions come with full financial support.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemistry, or related field
  • Strong academic record
  • Demonstrated interest in battery research and energy storage
  • Good communication skills in English
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively

Preferred Background

  • Experience with electrochemical testing or materials synthesis
  • Familiarity with characterization techniques (XRD, SEM, spectroscopy)
  • Programming skills (Python, MATLAB, etc.)
  • Prior research experience (undergraduate thesis, internships)

What We Offer

  • Full funding package (tuition waiver + monthly stipend)
  • Access to cutting-edge research facilities
  • Opportunities for synchrotron experiments at national facilities
  • Mentorship and career development support
  • Collaborative research environment
  • Support for conference participation and publications

Rolling Admission

We welcome applications from prospective Master's students interested in battery research. Projects are available in all-solid-state batteries, lithium-sulfur systems, and advanced characterization methods. Funding may be available for qualified candidates.

Undergraduate Research Opportunities

Year-Round

Position Description

Undergraduate students at Taiwan Tech are welcome to join our research group. Opportunities are available for independent study, summer research, and senior thesis projects. This is an excellent way to gain hands-on research experience in battery science and prepare for graduate studies.

What You'll Learn

  • Battery cell assembly and testing procedures
  • Materials synthesis and characterization
  • Data analysis and scientific communication
  • Research methodology and experimental design

How to Apply

Application Materials

Please send the following materials to Prof. Huang at [email protected]:

  • CV/Resume: Including education, research experience, publications, and relevant skills
  • Cover Letter: Briefly describe your research interests, background, and why you want to join our group
  • Transcripts: Unofficial transcripts are acceptable for initial review
  • Publication List: If applicable (for postdoc and PhD applicants)
  • References: Names and contact information for 2-3 references (letters will be requested if needed)

Application Review

Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interviews. If you do not receive a response within two weeks, please consider that your application has not been selected for further consideration at this time.

Questions?

Feel free to reach out with any questions about research opportunities, application procedures, or life at Taiwan Tech.

About Taiwan Tech

National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (Taiwan Tech) is a premier technical university located in Taipei, Taiwan. The Department of Chemical Engineering offers world-class facilities and a collaborative research environment.

Why Taiwan Tech?

  • Top-ranked engineering university in Taiwan
  • Located in Taipei, a vibrant international city with excellent quality of life
  • Access to National Synchrotron Radiation Research Center (NSRRC)
  • Strong collaborations with industry and academia
  • International research environment with students from around the world

Living in Taipei

Taipei offers an excellent quality of life with affordable living costs, efficient public transportation, world-class food scene, and a safe, welcoming environment for international students and researchers.
