Operando X-ray Spectroscopy Reveals Dynamic Evolution of Cathode-Electrolyte Interface in Solid-State Batteries
Huang, C.J., Wang, Y.L., Chen, H.M., Lin, P.H., et al.
Nature Energy 2024 IF: 56.7
Abstract

Understanding interfacial dynamics in all-solid-state batteries is crucial for advancing this technology. We employed operando X-ray absorption spectroscopy to reveal real-time chemical and structural evolution at the cathode-electrolyte interface during battery cycling.
