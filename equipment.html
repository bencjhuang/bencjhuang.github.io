Facilities & access

Beamline access today, in-house instrumentation as the lab is established at Taiwan Tech.

01 · Overview

Where we work

The group launches at Taiwan Tech in August 2026. In-house lab instrumentation will come online progressively as funding is secured — covering electrochemical cycling, argon-glovebox cell assembly, materials synthesis, and solid-state cell processing (planetary milling, blade-free mixing, cold isostatic pressing).

The work the group is known for, however, happens at synchrotron light sources. We maintain established access at Taiwan's NSRRC and an active collaboration at the Advanced Photon Source at Argonne for advanced X-ray characterization of working batteries.

02 · Beamline access

Synchrotron facilities

Direct access Hsinchu, Taiwan

NSRRC — National Synchrotron Radiation Research Center

Beamtime for operando X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS), X-ray diffraction (XRD), small-angle scattering (SAXS), and transmission X-ray microscopy (TXM) on battery samples under realistic operating conditions.

  • XAS
  • XRD
  • SAXS
  • XRF
  • TXM

Collaboration Lemont, IL · USA

APS — Advanced Photon Source, Argonne National Laboratory

Through ongoing collaboration with the imaging group at APS and Prof. Shirley Meng's group at the University of Chicago, we access world-class X-ray micro-computed tomography and high-energy synchrotron beamlines for 3D non-destructive battery characterization.

  • µXCT
  • High-energy XRD
  • Nano-tomography
