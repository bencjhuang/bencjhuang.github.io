Facilities & instrumentation

Synchrotron access today, in-house instrumentation as the lab is established at Taiwan Tech.

01 · Overview

Where we work

The group launches at Taiwan Tech in August 2026. The in-house instrumentation listed below is planned for the lab and will come online progressively as funding is secured.

The work the group is known for, however, happens at synchrotron light sources. We maintain established access at Taiwan's NSRRC and an active collaboration at the Advanced Photon Source at Argonne for advanced X-ray characterization of working batteries.

02 · Electrochemistry

Electrochemical characterization

Battery cycler

Coming soon

Multi-channel testing for galvanostatic cycling, rate capability, and long-term studies.

  • Current ranges: 5 ranges, 0.2 µA – 100 mA
  • Voltage range: ±5 V

Potentiostat — Biologic VSP-300

Coming soon

Precision electrochemical workstation: cyclic voltammetry, linear sweep, chronoamperometry, and EIS.

  • Voltage range: ±10 V down to ±30 mV
  • Resolution: 1 µV (±60 mV) – 333 µV (±10 V)
  • Current range: ±1 A – 10 nA
  • EIS range: 11 MHz – 10 µHz
03 · Synthesis & prep

Materials synthesis & preparation

Argon-filled glovebox

Coming soon

Inert-atmosphere workstation for moisture- and oxygen-sensitive material handling, cell assembly, and solid-electrolyte processing.

  • H2O, O2 < 1 ppm
  • Heatable antechamber
  • Working gas: Argon

Planetary mixer — Thinky ARM-310

Coming soon

Blade-free centrifugal mixer for homogeneous slurry preparation and dry powder mixing — bubble-free results without manual intervention.

  • Rotation speed: 200–2000 RPM (step), 2000 RPM (fixed)
  • Mixing: Non-vacuum, planetary rotation + revolution

Cold isostatic press (CIP)

Coming soon

Pouch-cell densification by uniform isostatic pressure through a hydraulic medium. Unlike uniaxial pressing, the pressure transmits equally from all directions for uniform compaction.

  • Temperature: room temperature
  • Maximum pressure: 500 MPa
  • Sample diameter: 50 mm
  • Cell type: pouch
  • Pressure medium: hydraulic oil

Planetary ball mill — Retsch PM 200

Coming soon

High-energy milling for solid-electrolyte synthesis, composite-cathode preparation, and mechanochemical synthesis of battery materials.

  • Grinding stations: 2
  • Speed: 100–650 RPM
  • Speed ratio: 1:2
  • Jar size: 25 mL
04 · Beamline access

Synchrotron facilities

Direct access Hsinchu, Taiwan

NSRRC — National Synchrotron Radiation Research Center

Beamtime for operando X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS), X-ray diffraction (XRD), small-angle scattering (SAXS), and transmission X-ray microscopy (TXM) on battery samples under realistic operating conditions.

  • XAS
  • XRD
  • SAXS
  • XRF
  • TXM
nsrrc.org.tw →
Collaboration Lemont, IL · USA

APS — Advanced Photon Source, Argonne National Laboratory

Through ongoing collaboration with the imaging group at APS and Prof. Shirley Meng's group at the University of Chicago, we access world-class X-ray micro-computed tomography and high-energy synchrotron beamlines for 3D non-destructive battery characterization.

  • µXCT
  • High-energy XRD
  • Nano-tomography
aps.anl.gov →
