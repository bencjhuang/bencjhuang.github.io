Mission Statement

At the CJHuang Group, we are dedicated to bridging the gap between fundamental materials science and practical energy storage engineering. Our research focuses on the design and synthesis of next-generation battery chemistries—specifically All-Solid-State Batteries (ASSBs), Lithium-Sulfur (Li-S) systems, and Anode-Free Lithium Metal Batteries.

We distinguish ourselves by integrating advanced multi-scale characterization techniques with material development. By utilizing operando spectroscopy and 3D imaging (X-ray tomography), we aim to visualize and understand the complex electrochemical interfaces that govern battery performance and failure.

🔋

All-Solid-State Batteries

Developing next-generation solid-state battery architectures with enhanced safety and energy density through advanced interface engineering.

⚛️

in-situ/operando Techniques

Real-time characterization of battery materials during operation using synchrotron X-ray techniques to understand dynamic electrochemical processes.

⚗️

3D X-ray Tomography

Visualizing battery microstructure and failure mechanisms in three dimensions to guide materials design and optimization.
